About the Service

What is
Field Collection?

Greatinco’s Field Collection service is designed to support recovery activities through professional on-site visits, debtor verification, negotiation support, and structured digital reporting. By combining experienced field collectors with mobile collection technology, we help clients improve collection effectiveness with enhanced operational visibility.

  • Structured field visit execution
  • Debtor verification and on-site validation
  • Negotiation support and settlement facilitation
  • Digital field reporting for faster updates

Professional Execution

Field collectors operate based on clear procedures with measurable activity monitoring.

Technology Support

All field activities are supported by digital tools to enhance transparency and reporting.

Data Continuity

Debtor history and prior handling details support more effective field collection strategies.

Flexible Model

Coverage areas and handling capacity can be customized based on region, portfolio size, and client business targets.

Mobile Technology

Mobile Apps for
More Controlled Field Operations

Greatinco’s mobile apps enable field teams to execute visits, update case handling, and monitor productivity in real time. With digital system support, field operations become faster, more measurable, and transparent.

  • Real-time visit tracking and account logging via collector mobile devices.
  • Productivity and performance dashboards to monitor collector output and results.
  • Payment management system with proof-of-payment uploads, notifications, and photo documentation.
  • Debtor handling history to support more precise collection strategies.
  • Digital field documentation to improve reporting accuracy and transparency.
  • Faster data synchronization to central dashboards for operational monitoring.
  • Instant visit result updates for quicker follow-up actions.
