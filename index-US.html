Partnership Process

Concise, clear, and designed for B2B collaboration.

1

BUSINESS PRESENTATION

The partnership process begins with a pitching session, where our team introduces the company and presents an overview of our products and services, while also gaining a general understanding of the client’s needs.

2

NDA SIGNING

Following initial interest, the process continues with the signing of a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) as a commitment to maintaining confidentiality between both parties.

3

BUSINESS MATCHING

Next is the business matching phase, where we align our services with the client’s needs. This includes product analysis, service type determination, workforce planning, and cost estimation.

4

PROPOSAL

Based on this stage, our team will prepare a proposal outlining the recommended services along with a competitive and transparent pricing scheme.

5

CONTRACT FINALIZATION

The final stage is the signing of the partnership agreement, preceded by a document review by both parties to ensure all terms are aligned, followed by formal execution of the agreement.
